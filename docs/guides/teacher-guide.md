# Teacher guide

## Sign in

Open the private invitation from the school and use Google or a one-time email code. Use the same email address the school invited. If Common Time says the login is not linked to a teacher record, ask the owner to finish the Staff setup.

## Use My schedule

**My schedule** contains the teacher’s own calendar, pending proposals, weekly availability, upcoming lessons, and recent lessons. Open a lesson to review its place and notes, propose or apply a new time, report a cancellation, or record the outcome after it ends.

If a time change requires owner approval, the original lesson remains on the calendar until the owner accepts the proposal.

## Availability

Weekly availability tells the scheduler when lessons normally fit. Changing availability never moves an existing lesson. Some schools let teachers maintain their own availability; otherwise the owner controls it.

## Lesson outcomes

After a lesson ends, record what actually happened: completed, partially completed, student cancellation, teacher cancellation, school cancellation, or no-show. Outcomes affect later policy and billing review, so record the real event rather than the desired financial result.
