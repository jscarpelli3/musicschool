# Family and payer guide

## Open the family portal

Go directly to `https://app.commontime.studio/portal`. Enter the exact email address your school keeps on the family record, then enter the one-time code from your email. There is no password to remember.

The portal shows only records connected to that verified payer email: upcoming lessons, sent statements, approval and payment status, private calendar-subscription controls, and active automatic-payment permission.

If one email is attached ambiguously to more than one family record, the portal stops and asks you to contact the school rather than guessing which family to show.

## Review a statement

The school may send a private link for one exact statement. Check the school, family, period, line items, and total. Holding the approval control records permission for that amount; it does not charge a card.

If something is wrong, send the statement back with the closest reason and a short explanation. The old link stops working if the school replaces the statement.

## Stay safe

- Common Time application links use `app.commontime.studio`.
- A Common Time approval page never asks you to type a card number.
- If a message seems suspicious, type `https://app.commontime.studio/portal` into the browser yourself and compare the statement there.
- Contact the school named in the message if the amount, student, or timing does not match.

## Calendar and automatic payment

A private calendar subscription can add the family’s lessons to another calendar app. Treat its subscription URL like a password; rotate or revoke it if it is shared accidentally.

Automatic payment is separate from saving a payment method and separate from approving one statement. When enabled, it shows the monthly maximum and advance-notice period. It can be revoked from the portal for future statements.
