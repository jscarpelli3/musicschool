# School owner guide

## Add people

Use **Students**, **Families**, or **Staff**, then choose the matching Add button. When adding a student, add or choose the payer at the same time so scheduling and billing stay connected. A person’s name is a link wherever it appears; use it to open the full student, family, or teacher record.

Teacher invitations are private and tied to the teacher’s email. A teacher signs in without creating a password. If an invitation fails, the Staff record shows its delivery state and recovery options.

## Schedule lessons

The Dashboard’s week calendar is the main scheduling view.

1. Choose **Add lesson +** above the calendar or **New lesson +** in navigation.
2. Select the student, lesson type, teacher, place, date, and time.
3. If the time is outside the teacher’s availability, follow the approval path shown by the app instead of treating it as a confirmed lesson.
4. To change an existing lesson, open its block and use the reschedule controls. The history keeps the original and replacement relationship.

**Lessons to schedule** are owed lessons that do not currently have a time, such as an approved make-up. Scheduling one uses the owed lesson rather than silently creating an unrelated charge.

## Review requests

The **Approvals** button shows recent schedule or cancellation requests. **View all** opens the full queue. Review the student, teacher, original time, proposed change, and policy information before deciding. A pending proposal does not move the original lesson.

## Prepare a family statement

1. Open **Families**, choose a family, and prepare the month.
2. Resolve any lessons whose outcome or price is incomplete.
3. Review every line. Add a clearly explained adjustment or credit only when needed.
4. Lock the statement. This freezes the exact amount.
5. Send it to the payer for review.
6. If the payer sends it back, correct the existing statement, lock the replacement, and send the new version. The old link remains unusable.

The invoice states mean:

- **Draft / In review:** still editable by the school.
- **Locked:** frozen and ready to send.
- **Waiting for payer:** sent and awaiting a decision.
- **Approved:** payer accepted the exact amount; no payment has necessarily happened.
- **Collecting / Paid / Payment failed:** actual payment progress.

## Notifications and provider uncertainty

Notifications report payer decisions and delivery problems. If an email provider times out, Common Time marks the result as uncertain instead of assuming failure and sending a duplicate. Check the record before retrying. Retry controls keep the same provider identity where required.

## School settings

Choose the gear beside the school name. School settings are visually separate from daily work and include contact details and the email address that receives replies; lesson and class types, prices, duration, and billing timing; rooms and lesson places; cancellation and family-access policies; and appearance.
