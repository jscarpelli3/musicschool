# Common Time beta quick start

Common Time keeps a school’s people, lesson calendar, schedule changes, family statements, payer approvals, and payment records connected. The private beta is invitation-only.

## Set up a new school

1. Open the private invitation link on the device and browser you plan to use.
2. Confirm the invited email with the one-time code. A one-time code is a short password that works once.
3. Enter the school name and timezone.
4. Add the school logo and your own avatar if you have them. Both can be changed later.
5. Add the first student and the person responsible for that student’s billing. Common Time calls that person the **payer**; it may be a parent, guardian, or an adult student.
6. Add the instruments your school teaches, then invite teachers. Teachers can also be added later.
7. Read the billing overview and finish setup.

Finishing requires a first student and payer. A logo, avatar, instrument list, and additional teachers are useful but do not block setup.

## Learn the owner workspace

- **Dashboard:** lessons that need scheduling, the main weekly calendar, the School overview, and recent invoice activity.
- **Students:** a simple student directory. Open a student for details and their lesson calendar.
- **Families:** payer and billing-account records. Open a family to prepare and review statements.
- **Staff:** teacher records, invitations, settings, availability, and assigned lessons.
- **Invoices:** recent statements and their current state.
- **Approvals:** schedule and cancellation decisions waiting for the owner.
- **Notifications:** important activity and delivery problems.
- **Gear beside the school name:** school information, lessons and classes, spaces, policies, and appearance.

## Important billing words

- A **draft statement** is an itemized proposed bill. It is editable.
- **Locking** freezes that exact version so it cannot quietly change while a payer reviews it.
- **Approval** is permission for one exact statement and amount. Approval does not charge a card.
- **Automatic payment permission** is a separate choice made by the payer. It can include a monthly limit and advance-notice period.
- A **payment** is the actual movement of money. A **receipt** exists only after a successful payment.

The beta does not yet execute live charges. Scheduling, statement preparation, approval, saved-payment-method setup, and payment records can be tested without charging a customer.

## If something looks wrong

- Do not repeat an action blindly if the app says the provider outcome is uncertain. Check Notifications and the record first.
- A payer who distrusts an email should open `https://app.commontime.studio/portal` directly and compare the school, period, amount, and status.
- Legitimate Common Time application links use `app.commontime.studio`.
- Contact Common Time before importing real customer data or attempting a live charge during the beta.
